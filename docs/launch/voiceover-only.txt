1. Every business already has the data to answer its most important questions. The problem is, those answers get stuck — buried in dashboards nobody has time to explore, or waiting weeks in a consultant's queue. What if you could just ask, and a whole team got to work?

2. This is AgentForge. I type the mission in plain language: analyze why our churn spiked, and recommend three fixes. Then I attach our customer CSV — two thousand rows of plans, usage, and churn flags. I pick a team, hit launch, and AgentForge staffs the job: a planner, a researcher, an analyst, a forecaster, and a critic.

3. The planner goes first, breaking the mission into a DAG of subtasks and assigning each to a specialist.

4. The researcher pulls outside context — churn benchmarks and industry reports — and summarizes what it finds.

5. Meanwhile the analyst opens my CSV, profiles the columns, and runs pandas — landing real tables and charts in the feed.

6. The forecaster projects what's next; every prediction carries its own confidence and its basis.

7. Then the critic. It just flagged a claim that was too strong, downgraded it, and forced another ranking pass — nothing unreviewed reaches the report.

8. That meter tracks every token per agent with running cost — no black box. And this run is demo mode: deterministic simulated agents, so it plays the same offline; live mode swaps in the real model.

9. Thirty-five seconds after launch, the report is ready. It opens with an executive summary, then the insights, ranked by evidence strength. The top one: basic-plan customers churn at thirty-seven point four percent — almost three times the company average of just under twenty-five.

10. Here's the driver: churned customers had a median of thirteen logins last month; active ones, forty-two. The forecaster scores who's at risk next. Then my three fixes, ranked by impact versus effort: save offers when usage drops, an onboarding push early, and annual plans for basic customers.

11. No need to rebuild this by hand. The template marketplace ships three ready teams — market research, sales insights, support triage. And any finished run exports in one click as an n8n workflow — import it into n8n Cloud and wire it into your stack.

12. That's AgentForge — hired with one sentence. Next I'd build step-level retries from n8n and a hosted live-mode deployment. Thanks for watching.
